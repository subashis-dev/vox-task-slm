⚙️ Required 2-Minute PC Setup for Wake-on-LAN:

  1. In PC BIOS (press DEL on boot): Go to Advanced → APM Configuration and enable "Power On By PCI-E / PME".
  2. In Windows Device Manager: Open Network Adapters → Your Intel/Realtek Ethernet Card → Properties → Power Management. Check "Allow this device to wake the computer" and "Only allow a magic packet to wake the computer".
  3. Get Your PC's MAC Address: Open Command Prompt and type getmac. That's the address we'll put in the ESP32 code!

⚡ How to Keep the ESP32 Powered When PC is Off

By default, modern PCs cut USB power when shut down to save power. To keep your ESP32 on, enter BIOS and set "ErP Ready" → Disabled. This leaves +5V Standby power active on your motherboard USB ports!

BIOS: ErP = Disabled
